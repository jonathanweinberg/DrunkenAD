# Contributing To DrunkenAD

Thank you for considering a contribution.

## Contributor Agreement

Before a contribution can be accepted, you must agree to the
[DrunkenAD Contributor License Agreement](CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md).

By opening a pull request, submitting a patch, or otherwise providing material
for inclusion, you confirm that you have the right to submit the contribution
and that it can be licensed under the contributor agreement.

Contributions made on behalf of an employer, client, or other organization may
require written authorization from that organization before they can be accepted.

## License

Unless otherwise stated in a file, accepted contributions are distributed under
the repository's [BSD 3-Clause License](LICENSE).

## Contribution Hygiene

- Do not include passwords, tokens, private keys, certificates, customer data,
  private directory exports, or live lab credentials.
- Keep generated run output out of commits unless a maintainer asks for a
  specific sanitized artifact.
- Prefer focused pull requests with a clear purpose and validation notes.
- Update documentation when behavior or operator workflow changes.
- Add or update tests when changing module behavior.
